Third-party software in this app
================================

The app uses the libraries below, in Contents/Frameworks. Each library's own
licence files are in Contents/Resources/licenses/<library>/. Thank you to all
of their authors.


Each was built unmodified from its official release source.

https://github.com/icculus/SDL_sound. Copyright (c) 2001-2026 Ryan C. Gordon

FFmpeg (libavformat, libavcodec, libswscale, libavutil), version 8.1.2
---------------------------------------------------------------------
https://ffmpeg.org/. The FFmpeg developers. GNU Lesser General Public
License, version 2.1 or later (licenses/<library>/ffmpeg-8.1.2/). Used by
mw3import, which converts your disc's movies on the first start. Built
unmodified from ffmpeg.org's release tarball ffmpeg-8.1.2.tar.xz, with only
the AVI demuxer, the Indeo 5 decoder, the MPEG-1 video encoder and muxer,
swscale and the file protocol enabled (no GPL or non-free parts); the
configure line is in the project's docs/deps.md. The libraries are loaded
dynamically from Contents/Frameworks: you may replace them with your own
build of the same version.
