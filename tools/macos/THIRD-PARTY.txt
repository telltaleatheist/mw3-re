Third-party software in this app
================================

The app uses the libraries below, in Contents/Frameworks. Each library's own
licence files are in Contents/Resources/licenses/<library>/. Thank you to all
of their authors.

---------------

SDL_ttf 3 (libSDL3_ttf)
-----------------------
https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL_ttf. Copyright (C) 1997-2025 Sam Lantinga
and others. zlib licence. It loads:
- FreeType (libfreetype), under the FreeType License (FTL),
  https://freetype.org/license.html. Portions of this software are
  copyright (C) The FreeType Project (www.freetype.org). All rights
  reserved.
- HarfBuzz (libharfbuzz), MIT licence ("Old MIT"),
  https://github.com/harfbuzz/harfbuzz, which loads graphite2 (libgraphite2,
  LGPL 2.1 or later, or MPL 2.0, https://github.com/silnrsi/graphite) and
  GLib (libglib-2.0, LGPL 2.1 or later, https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/glib),
  which loads GNU gettext's libintl (LGPL 2.1 or later,
  https://www.gnu.org/software/gettext/) and PCRE2 (libpcre2-8, BSD,
  https://github.com/PCRE2Project/pcre2).
- libpng (libpng16), the libpng licence, http://www.libpng.org/.
The LGPL libraries are unmodified Homebrew builds, loaded dynamically from
Contents/Frameworks; you may replace them with your own builds of the same
versions. Their source: the project links above, and Homebrew's formulae
(https://github.com/Homebrew/homebrew-core), which name the exact versions.
